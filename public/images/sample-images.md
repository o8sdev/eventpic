# SnapMatch sample photographs

These fictional wedding photographs were made with the built-in image generation tool. They are public marketing examples, never guest uploads or evidence of real matches. The gallery labels them as illustrative AI images. Originals remain outside the repository; the three selected assets are encoded as WebP for delivery.

- `wedding-reception.webp`: a candid moment at the reception.
- `wedding-dance.webp`: the couple's first dance.
- `wedding-friends.webp`: the couple celebrating with two adult friends.
